About Bandozee Traditional Attire

Bandozee is a Durban-based traditional attire company dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of South Africa through authentic, high-quality clothing and accessories.

Founded in 2010, our mission has always been to provide beautifully crafted traditional garments that celebrate our diverse cultures while incorporating contemporary elements for modern appeal.

Our Values

  • Cultural preservation and celebration
  • Exceptional craftsmanship and quality
  • Authenticity in design and materials
  • Community empowerment through job creation
  • Sustainable and ethical production practices

Our Craftspeople

All our products are handmade by skilled local artisans who have mastered traditional techniques passed down through generations. We work directly with communities across KwaZulu-Natal to ensure fair wages and preserve these important cultural skills.
